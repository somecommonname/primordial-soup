# 🧫 Primordial Soup

**A small evolving universe in one HTML file. Now v1.9.**

Creatures with 15 genes and a 108 weight neural brain swim in a petri dish where they eat, hunt, court, breed with genetic crossover, speciate, remember, speak, and leave scent trails. Nothing is scripted: predators, herds, jackal omnivores, quilled defenders, cold-adapted peoples, and wartime brains all *evolved* in testing rather than being programmed. You play the environment.

## Play

**Just open the URL.** The game runs entirely in your browser. No download, no account, no server, no frameworks: one file.

Your dish **persists**: it auto-saves as you play, evolution continues while you are away, and your return triggers a visible fast-forward. Install it as an app from the address bar; it works offline and updates itself. Works on phones: panels fold to their headers, tap to expand.

## What the creatures are

- **🧬 15 genes**: size, speed, vision, diet, fear, fertility, herding, wander, spikes, shell, comfort zone, immunity, brain power, plus color as lineage and mate compatibility
- **🧠 A real evolvable brain**: 108 weights, mutated and crossed over like any gene, paid for in energy
- **💭 Memory**: two recurrent cells; fear can outlast its cause
- **📣 A voice and ears, 🐜 scent trails**: physical channels only; what any signal means must evolve
- **💕 Sexual reproduction**: courtship, crossover, and true species boundaries; drift far enough apart and lineages can no longer interbreed
- **🦔 Quills and shells** with honest tradeoffs; **🦠 parasites** with evolving virulence against costly immunity

## The world

- **🌿 Plants** grow and spread themselves in patches; corpses become seeds
- **🪨 Rocks** block movement and sight; ambush predation is real
- **🌊 Currents** sweep the small; the big resist
- **🌡 Climate**: a warm end, a cold end, and a four minute year with seasons
- **🌍 Four world types**: Classic, Ice World, Eden, Plaguelands

## You

- **🔍 inspect** any creature; **✎ name** it; **☆ follow** its bloodline forever; **🎴** turn it into a trading card
- **Blessings**: 🌿 bloom, 🧬 seed strangers, 🧪 elixir (heal and cure)
- **Catastrophes**: ☄️ meteor, 🦠 plague, ❄️ ice age
- **🪨 Sculpt terrain** by hand; **☀️ sunlight** and **☢️ radiation** dials
- **❓ guide** in the panel teaches all of this in plain bullets

## It remembers everything

- **🌳 Tree of life**: a live phylogeny streamgraph; branches split as species diverge, fade as they die
- **📜 The Chronicle**: the dish writes its own yearly history
- **🏆 Records and 🏅 23 badges** that survive every world
- **🆔 Every dish has a callsign** like SOUP-K7F3-9Q

## Sharing

- **⇪ Export** your whole world as a compressed seed string; **⇩ import** anyone else's, fossil record intact
- **📷 Postcards** and **🎴 creature cards** with stats, records, and badges, straight to your share sheet

## Field notes

This project's best moments were diagnoses: creatures that orbited food they could not reach, a predator that starved while touching its prey, a dish rim that was accidentally a merry-go-round sanctuary, and brains that tripled under predation then shrank in peacetime. The debugging stories are preserved in the roadmap: bugs are field notes.

Performance is a standing rule: the whole simulation runs in well under a millisecond per step, and every release reports its numbers.
